Easy and Fast Bread

Description

This versatile, hassle-free artisan bread recipe is designed to fit seamlessly into any schedule, whether you need a fresh homemade loaf in a single afternoon or prefer to let the flavors develop over time. Using simple pantry staples and a quick hand-mixed method that requires no tedious kneading, the dough comes together effortlessly in a single container. Once mixed, you enjoy total flexibility: allow it to rise at room temperature for a quick same-day bake, or store it in the refrigerator for up to seven days, baking off a hot, fresh loaf whenever you wish.

Baking the cold dough in a preheated Dutch oven yields an extraordinary, bakery-quality result with a deeply golden, beautifully blistered crust, dramatic flour-dusted slashes, and a rich, crackly texture. The steam trapped beneath the lid ensures maximum oven spring, creating a wonderfully light crumb beneath that rustic exterior. Perfect for novice bakers and seasoned kitchen pros alike, this reliable recipe guarantees a striking, gourmet loaf with minimal hands-on effort every single time.

Ingredients

  • 475 g warm water, 86–90 °F
  • 7 g instant yeast
  • 20g sugar
  • 20 g neutral oil
  • 15 g salt
  • 500 g all-purpose flour *See Notes*
  • 150 g whole-wheat flour
  • 30 g ground flaxseed

Steps

  1. Combine the warm water in a bowl and stir in the yeast and sugar. Let it foam 5 minutes.
  2. During this time, combine in a tall, lidded 6 qt container, the all-purpose flour, whole-wheat flour, ground flaxseed and salt.
  3. Stir in the oil to the yeast mixture.
  4. Add the yeast mixture to the other ingredients and stir with a spoon for 30 secondes until a shaggy mass forms. Wet your hand and squeeze the dough until it is homogenous.
  5. Cover and rest at room temperature for 20-25 minutes or until the dough has risen by about 50%.
  6. Refrigerate overnight (or ferment 90 minutes more at room temperature for same-day use). The dough can stay in the fridge up to 7 days.
  7. Flip cold dough onto a lightly floured surface, sticky side up. Stretch‐and‐fold the left and right sides, then the remaining corners. Flip seam-side down and tighten by tucking and rolling 20–30 times.
  8. Dust a proofing basket and the loaf with flour. Place seam-side up; proof 45–75 minutes at room temperature
  9. Preheat a Dutch oven to 475 °F (25 minutes).
  10. Turn loaf seam-side down into the hot pot, dust with semolina, score six 1-inch-deep slashes, cover, reduce oven temperature to 450 °F and bake 22 minutes. Remove lid; bake 20 minutes more. Cool on a wire rack.

Note 1 The three types of flour can simply be replaced with 680 g of all-purpose flour to make a classic white loaf.

Note 2 This bread is best consumed within 3 days of baking.
